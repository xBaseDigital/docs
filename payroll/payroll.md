# Payroll

Relm Payroll lets businesses distribute payments to multiple beneficiaries across
different currencies from a single source account, with transparent fees and a
quote-based approval workflow.

There are two ways to integrate:

- **[Payroll API](/payroll/api)** — Server-side integration for estimating fees,
  creating payroll requests, managing quotes, and tracking beneficiary payouts.
- **[Payroll Widget](/payroll/widget)** — Drop-in embeddable widget for adding
  payroll distribution to your product with minimal setup.
