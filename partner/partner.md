# Partner API

APIs for partners to manage and onboard their own customers onto the Relm platform.

- **[Onboard Customer](/partner/onboard-customer)** — create and link a new customer to your partner account, to be used before launching Onboarding SDK.
- **[Manage Client Banking](/partner/manage-client)** — manage accounts, currencies, beneficiaries, whitelisting, and payouts on behalf of a customer you own.
- **[Manage Client Payroll](/partner/manage-client-payroll)** — estimate fees, create and manage payroll requests, and track beneficiary transfers on behalf of a customer you own.
